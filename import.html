Import from photo

Upload one or more photos of a recipe — a cookbook page, a handwritten card, or a series of screenshots (e.g. a Pinterest pin or a blog post). Claude will read them and pull out a clean, ad-free recipe for you to review before saving.

Selecting more than one sends them together, as parts of the same recipe — e.g. one photo of the ingredients and another of the steps.
